# Free-browsing platforms for choosing a design direction

Use when asking for the user's initial style input, especially when they do not know how to describe their taste. Offer two or three relevant links, not an exhaustive directory. The user can instead describe a direction or share a reference they already have. Browsing these platforms is optional; supplying an aesthetic basis before design is required under [discovery](discovery.md#required-style-input-before-design).

## Choose by the kind of reference needed

| Platform | Useful for | Free-browsing scope and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja/) | Real websites, landing pages, SaaS, portfolios, typography, and full-page composition | Its [about page](https://www.lapa.ninja/about/) describes thousands of curated pages and free search without an account. [Pro](https://www.lapa.ninja/pro/) adds collection and browsing conveniences; it is not required for the suggested reference-search workflow. |
| [Siteinspire](https://www.siteinspire.com/) | Website composition, editorial typography, restrained or expressive layouts, and portfolios | Its public gallery exposes a large archive with style/type/subject filters and pagination. Use the public examples and original-site links; do not promise that every current or future feature is free. |
| [Behance UI/UX projects](https://www.behance.net/search/projects/ui%20ux?locale=en_US) | App/web UI, longer project presentations, brand direction, typography, and illustration | Public search offers a large body of viewable projects. Paid assets, subscriptions, saving, and account features are separate from public project viewing. A sign-in or regional restriction may still affect a specific visit. |
| [Dribbble Product Design](https://dribbble.com/shots/popular/product-design) / [Web Design](https://dribbble.com/shots/popular/web-design) | UI screens, dashboards, controls, color, and local visual treatment | Public galleries show design shots without requiring a purchase in the inspected results. Some paths or actions may prompt sign-up; do not promise unlimited anonymous access. Shots can be concepts rather than shipped products or complete flows. |

For a website, start with Lapa Ninja and Siteinspire. For an app or dashboard, start with Behance and Dribbble. For brand or illustration direction, Behance is a useful starting point. Choose by the user's task, not a platform's popularity.

## Help the user bring back useful input

Ask them to send a link or screenshot they like and, if possible, say what draws them to it: overall feel, typography, color, spacing, composition, or a specific component. One useful example can be enough; do not set a mandatory reference count or require a mood board.

If they cannot name the part, accept plain language and ask a focused question after inspecting the example. "I like its overall feel" is also useful. A link supplied for another purpose is not automatically a style choice. A platform homepage by itself does not identify which design the user likes; ask them to pick an example or describe a direction.

Do not infer that the user likes every part of a selected example, the first search result, or a platform's prevailing style. Public viewing is not a license to reuse assets. Screenshots establish appearance, not verified behavior, motion, or usability.

If a link is blocked, paid, unavailable, or requires an account the user does not want to create, offer another public platform or accept a screenshot/description. Do not require payment, registration, software installation, or access-control workarounds. Do not replace the missing input with an agent-selected style.

## Sources and maintenance

Access and scale were checked on 2026-10-29 using official pages and public gallery/project views. These are observations at that time, not a guarantee of permanent free access. Recheck when recommending a new platform or when a user reports a changed restriction; a full platform audit is not needed for each design conversation.

- [Lapa Ninja about](https://www.lapa.ninja/about/) and [Pro](https://www.lapa.ninja/pro/): separate free discovery from paid collections and conveniences.
- [Siteinspire gallery](https://www.siteinspire.com/): public categories, examples, and pagination.
- [Behance discovery guide](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/204484044-Guide-Discover-Creative-Work-on-Behance) and [UI/UX project search](https://www.behance.net/search/projects/ui%20ux?locale=en_US): field-based discovery and public project results.
- [Dribbble product](https://dribbble.com/shots/popular/product-design) and [web](https://dribbble.com/shots/popular/web-design) galleries: publicly exposed shots and category navigation.

These services are optional places for the user to choose references, not ByTaste dependencies or sources of user authorization. They do not depend on the planned ByTaste reference library, CLI, or Pro service.

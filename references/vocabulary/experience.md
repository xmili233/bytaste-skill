# Experience goal categories

These terms help express the feelings and priorities a user wants to emphasize in this project. They are not basic-quality options to tick. The [default quality baseline](../visual-quality.md) already covers applicable clarity, low cognitive effort, control, and related goals. Deliver those proactively without imposing specific mechanisms or efficiency, conversion, and usability experiments.

Read as needed when users say "efficient," "professional," "reassuring," or similar without explaining what they mean. These 14 goals are combinable starting points for questions and organization, not a complete questionnaire or mutually exclusive types.

Speak through familiar situations before introducing goal names. Questions and rules below are candidates: reuse answers from context, and do not add nonexistent functionality merely because a goal suggests it.

| ID / goal | Possible user wording | Distinguishing question | Rules to consider after clarification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `goal.focus` Focus | No distractions, emphasize what matters, concentrate | What should not interrupt this activity? | Main-task hierarchy, timing of supporting information, distraction control |
| `goal.efficiency` Efficiency | Fast, smooth once familiar, fewer detours | Which action repeats, and where do you most want to avoid repeated work? | Frequent-action placement, retained context, existing batch/shortcut paths |
| `goal.clarity` Clarity | Understand at a glance, orderly, findable | What needs to be immediately clear: state, next step, or relationships? | Naming, grouping, hierarchy, state explanation |
| `goal.low-friction` Ease | Less thinking, fewer settings, simpler | Do you most want fewer choices, less entry, or less back-and-forth searching? | Defaults, information reuse, on-demand display; users retain important decisions |
| `goal.control` Control | Adjustable, predictable, do not change things for me | Which outcomes should be previewable, reversible, or manually adjustable? | Feedback, preview, revision, recovery paths |
| `goal.confidence` Confidence | Stable, reliable, safe to use | Are mistakes, inaccurate data, or uncertainty about success the main concern? | Consequences, source/time, execution state, error recovery |
| `goal.approachable` Approachability | Less cold, less intimidating, accessible | Should language feel more natural, or actions be easier to understand? | Specific wording, familiar interactions, visible help |
| `goal.professional` Rigor | Professional, credible, serious | Should this come from information accuracy, reliable flows, or visual order? | Data/copy precision, consistent behavior, stable typography |
| `goal.exploration` Exploration | Discovery, enjoyable browsing, trying things | Do users arrive with a clear goal, or discover interests while browsing? | Browse entries, relationship cues, reversible trials, return paths |
| `goal.expression` Expression | Distinctive, memorable, branded | Should users remember the work, brand graphics, voice, or another feature? | Expressive medium, emphasis scope, content/brand relationship |
| `goal.immersion` Immersion | Uninterrupted, absorbed, surrounded by content | Is the focus reading, appreciating work, or sustained operation? | Content share, control visibility, irrelevant interruptions |
| `goal.reading` Sustained reading | Comfortable, not tiring, inviting to read | Is the main activity continuous reading or locating a passage quickly? | Reading width, paragraph rhythm, contents/navigation, scanning hierarchy |
| `goal.comparison` Comparison | Side-by-side, information together, less flipping | What information needs to be visible together for a decision? | Alignment, consistent units, retained comparison context |
| `goal.guidance` Guidance | Walk me through it, do not let me get lost, beginner-friendly | Should help appear at every step or only when needed? | Step organization, contextual help, next/return entries |

## Tradeoffs among goals

Establish the users and pages to which each goal applies. Guidance for beginners and efficiency for experienced users may call for different paths. Immersion and control may coexist through contextual controls. Ask for priorities only when goals genuinely interfere in the same situation.

Experience goals do not determine visual style. Rigor can use different fonts and colors; approachability need not use cartoons. If appearance remains unclear, consult [visual dimensions](visual.md).

Once goals are clear, choose [candidate rules](../principles.md) for the task and add exceptions and checking methods.
